Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

The PAC ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use The PAC mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and username when you create an account.
  • Pet/animal data: Information you enter about your pets or farm animals, including health records, vaccination history, weight entries, and photos.
  • Location data: When you use the "Find Pet Services" feature, we access your device location to show nearby vets and services. This is only used in-app and never stored on our servers.
  • Usage data: Basic app usage information to improve the app experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate The PAC app and its features
  • To send you reminders about vaccinations, deworming, and health appointments
  • To power the AI Vet feature with context about your animal
  • To sync data across your devices and with co-parents you have added
  • To improve the app and fix issues

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Storage), hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell your data to any third party.

Third-Party Services

The PAC uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, database, and file storage
  • Google Maps & Places API: Vet and pet service discovery
  • Anthropic Claude API: AI Vet responses
  • Expo: App infrastructure and push notifications

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data we hold about you
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen
  • Remove co-parents from your pet's data at any time
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time

Children's Privacy

The PAC is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@thepac.in.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at:
privacy@thepac.in

Terms of Service

By using The PAC, you agree to use the app for lawful purposes only. The PAC provides health tracking tools and AI-powered guidance for informational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional veterinary advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified veterinarian for your animal's health needs.

We reserve the right to terminate accounts that misuse the service. We are not liable for any decisions made based on information provided by the app.
